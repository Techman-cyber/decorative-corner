Started in a garage. Still roasted by hand.

Ember & Ash began in 2019 with a secondhand roaster and a list of farms we wanted to work with directly. Six years on, the roaster's bigger but the approach hasn't changed: pay growers fairly, roast in small lots, and never let a bag sit in a warehouse.

Every bag lists the farm, the harvest date, and the roast date — so you always know what you're drinking and how fresh it is.
